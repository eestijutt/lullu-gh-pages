2. The App: General Principles

  • The App requires no account and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • The App shows no ads. It does not collect advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID or the iPhone IDFA), and App data is not used for advertising.
  • The Operator does not sell or rent App data to anyone.

3. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store on Lullu's server your device's notification token (Expo push token), the device type (iOS or Android), the App version and the IP address from which the token was registered. We use them to notify you when a new poem is published and to keep the notification system reliable and secure. Notifications are delivered by the Expo service through Apple's and Google's notification services.

We delete the token when the notification service reports that the App has been removed from the device. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App's settings or in your device settings.

4. Usage Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics statistics to see how much the App is used and which poems are read. We use the data to improve the user experience and to develop new features. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not linked to your name or contact details.

  • What is collected: how the App is used (e.g. poems opened and shared, features and settings used), device model, operating system and App version, language, and approximate location at country or city level.
  • How users are told apart: only by a random number that the App creates when it is installed. Reinstalling creates a new number. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.
  • Legal basis: the Operator's legitimate interest in understanding how the App is used and improving it.
  • Retention: up to 14 months.

5. Error Reports (Sentry)

When an error occurs in the App, an error report is sent to the Sentry service so that we can find and fix it. The report contains a description of the error, the App and operating system versions, the device model, a random ID created for the installation, approximate location (country and city, which Sentry derives from the connection) and the actions taken just before the error. For some sessions and errors, a screen recording is attached in which all text and images are masked. Sentry does not store the IP address, and the reports contain no name or contact details. The legal basis is the Operator's legitimate interest in keeping the App reliable. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

6. Sharing

When you share a poem, the App opens the app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram) and passes it the poem's image or link. That app then processes it under its own terms. On iPhone, the App uses Meta's software to open the Facebook share dialog. The App sends Meta no usage statistics or advertising identifiers, but Meta's software may connect to Meta's servers (e.g. to load its configuration), which lets Meta see the device's IP address.

7. Recipients and Security

  • App data is processed on the Operator's behalf by Google Ireland Limited (usage statistics), Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, error reports), 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo, notification delivery) and the hosting provider of Lullu's server.
  • Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area (USA). Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can turn notifications off in the App's settings or in your device settings.
  • Removing the App stops data collection, and its notification token is deleted.
  • You have the right to ask what data has been collected about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing and to object, including to the collection of usage statistics based on legitimate interest. Write to eesti@jutud.ee. If you believe your data is being processed incorrectly, you can contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate.

9. The lullu.ee Website

This section describes the data the Website collects. Google Analytics and the Meta pixel load only after you have given your consent in the cookie window. Without consent they do not load, and the Website sets only the cookie that remembers your choice.

The Website loads its fonts (Google Fonts) and the tag manager (Google Tag Manager) from Google's servers. Google therefore sees your IP address, but these set no cookies and collect no usage statistics.

What we collect and why

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics 4), with your consent: how many people visit the Website, which pages and poems are viewed, where visitors come from (e.g. an ad or social media) and how many tap the app download buttons. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.
  • Advertising (Meta pixel), with your consent: we measure how well Lullu ads on Facebook and Instagram work, and can show Lullu ads to people who have visited the Website. Meta learns that your browser visited the Website, which pages were viewed there and whether an app download button was tapped. Meta may link this data to your Facebook or Instagram account.
  • Campaign tag in the store links: the App Store and Google Play buttons pass the campaign name to the store (e.g. which ad or page you came from), so we can see how many downloads each campaign brought. It contains no personal data and needs no cookies.

Legal basis and withdrawing consent

The usage statistics and advertising cookies are based on your consent. You can change or withdraw it at any time with the link at the bottom of the page. When you withdraw consent, we delete the related cookies from your browser. Withdrawal does not affect processing based on consent given earlier.

Recipients

  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics) processes the usage statistics on the Operator's behalf.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Meta pixel). For collecting the data on the Website and transmitting it to Meta, the Operator and Meta are joint controllers. Meta is responsible for its further use of the data, see Meta's privacy policy.
  • Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area (USA). Google and Meta participate in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
